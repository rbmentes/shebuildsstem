📱 Social Media & Apps

🎵 TikTok
Do you know how TikTok decides which videos appear on your For You Page?
Tap to reveal
The Answer 🎵
TikTok uses a recommendation model that scores every video for you personally — based on how long you watch, whether you replay, like, comment, or skip. It doesn't care about follower counts at all.
💡 Even a brand new account with 0 followers can go viral overnight. The algorithm gives every video a fair shot with a small test audience first, then boosts it if people engage.
Recommender System
🎧 Spotify
Do you know why Spotify Discover Weekly feels like it reads your mind?
Tap to reveal
The Answer 🎧
Spotify uses collaborative filtering — it finds millions of users who have similar taste to you, then recommends songs they love that you haven't heard yet. It's not about genres, it's about listening behaviour patterns.
💡 Spotify also runs NLP on music blogs and lyrics to understand what words people associate with a song — so it knows a track is "late night" or "rainy day" without you ever saying so.
Collaborative Filtering + NLP
📸 Instagram
Do you know how Instagram can tell if a photo is AI-generated?
Tap to reveal
The Answer 📸
Instagram uses a classifier trained on millions of real vs AI-generated images. It looks for patterns invisible to humans — things like unnatural pixel distributions, perfect skin textures, and subtle lighting inconsistencies that AI models tend to leave behind.
💡 It also checks metadata embedded in the image file. Tools like DALL-E and Midjourney often tag their outputs with invisible watermarks — a bit like a digital fingerprint.
Image Classification + Watermarking
🎶 Shazam
Do you know how Shazam recognises a song from just 5 seconds of noise?
Tap to reveal
The Answer 🎶
Shazam converts your audio into a spectrogram — a visual fingerprint of the sound. It then extracts the strongest frequency peaks and creates a compact "constellation map." This map is matched against a database of billions of fingerprints in milliseconds.
💡 The genius part? It works even with background noise because it only looks at the loudest, most prominent frequency points — like identifying a face by just its eyes and nose, ignoring everything else.
Audio Fingerprinting + Spectrograms
🔍 Instagram
Do you know how Instagram's Explore page decides what to show you — without you searching for anything?
Tap to reveal
The Answer 🔍
Instagram scores every post in its database for you using signals like accounts you follow, posts you've saved, time spent on similar content, and what people like you tend to engage with — all without you typing a single word.
💡 Instagram runs this scoring for hundreds of millions of users simultaneously. It uses a two-stage system: first a fast rough filter, then a slower, more precise ranking — like a talent show with auditions before the final round.
Two-Stage Ranking System
